Puzzle App

Yenadoku

A stage-based sudoku app that puts large text, wide touch targets, and an easy input flow first. Continue playing and keep records with on-device storage only — no login required.

Support Privacy Policy Terms of Service
Core structure No login · local storage · accessibility-focused puzzle experience

Progress, settings, and best records are managed in the device's internal storage; no server accounts are used.

Features

Key features

Stage-based difficulty

Puzzles are organized into Easy, Normal, Hard, and Expert stages for easy browsing.

Large text and wide touch targets

Designed with a screen density that reduces input mistakes and keeps the focus on the puzzle.

Continue and auto-save

The current puzzle, records, and settings are saved locally so you can pick up where you left off.

Ad integration structure

Banner, interstitial, and rewarded ad units are part of the app structure, with production values injected at release.
