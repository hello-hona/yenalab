Where Is This? Privacy Policy

An app-specific policy for a login-free, local-storage world geography exploration app.

1. Overview

Where Is This? is a geography learning app offering world map exploration, country discovery, flag and capital quizzes, a collection book, and passport stamps. yenalab processes only the minimum information necessary to provide the service.

2. Sign-up and directly collected information

Where Is This? does not currently offer sign-up or login. We do not directly collect names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, contacts, precise location data, or camera data.

3. Information stored on the device

  • Discovered countries and discovery timestamps
  • Quiz types, correct and incorrect answer records, and last play time
  • Earned badges and passport stamp status
  • App settings such as language and text size

This information is stored in the device's internal storage by default. In the web version, it may be stored in browser storage or IndexedDB.

4. Third-party services

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob

Firebase Analytics may process event information such as country discoveries, quiz starts, quiz completions, and answer correctness to understand app usage. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, ad interaction data, and error data to deliver ads and run diagnostics in the Android app.

5. Child-directed ad settings

Android ads are configured for child-directed treatment, under-age-of-consent treatment, a general-audience ad rating, and non-personalized ad requests. The web version does not currently display ads.

6. Retention and deletion

Discovery records, quiz records, badges, and settings stored on the device may be deleted together when the app is uninstalled, app data is cleared, or browser site data is cleared. We do not provide server-account-based recovery.

7. Policy changes

This privacy policy may be updated when app features, ads, analytics tools, or storage methods change. Important changes will be announced through the app or the website.

8. Contact

For inquiries about personal information and data processing, please contact official@yenalab.com.
