Where Is This? Privacy Policy

This policy is written for a kids' world geography app that works without an account.

1. Overview

Where Is This? is a learning app for children. Kids follow picture clues to find countries and learn world geography with a pixel-art atlas, illustrated maps, quizzes and a flag matching game. yenalab processes only the minimum information needed to run and improve the app.

2. Information we do not collect

The app has no sign-up or sign-in. We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, contacts, photos or precise location. The app has no chat or user-generated content.

3. Information kept only on your device

  • Countries discovered and clues opened
  • Quiz and flag matching records
  • App settings such as language and text size

This information is stored only on your device (browser storage on the web version) and is never sent to yenalab's servers.

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. They process information under Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Firebase Analytics (Android, web): helps us see which features are used so we can improve the app. It processes app usage events such as screen views, countries discovered, clues recorded, atlas pictures and maps opened, quizzes started and completed with correct/incorrect answers, flag matching results and language changes. Events contain only app content values such as country codes, categories and scores, never names or free text. They may be processed together with a randomly generated app instance ID and basic information such as device model, OS version and approximate region (country level).
  • Google AdMob (Android): shows banner ads. For ad requests and diagnostics it may process the app set ID, IP address, device information, ad impressions and clicks, and error information. The web version shows no ads.

5. Settings for children

  • Ad requests are tagged as child-directed and under the age of consent, and only non-personalized ads rated G are requested. No interest-based ads or remarketing.
  • The Android advertising ID and related permissions are removed from the app, and advertising ID collection and ad personalization signals are turned off in Firebase Analytics.
  • Analytics data is not used for advertising and is never sold.

6. Retention and deletion

Records and settings on the device are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data (site data on the web). There is no account, so records cannot be restored from a server. Firebase Analytics data is deleted automatically under Google's retention setting (up to 14 months). To request deletion of analytics data, email us at the address below.

7. For parents and guardians

Parents and guardians can contact us at the email below with questions about their child's information or to request deletion. Because the app collects no personal information directly, uninstalling the app removes all records stored on the device in most cases.

8. Changes

If features, ads, analytics tools or storage change, we will update this policy and the date below.
