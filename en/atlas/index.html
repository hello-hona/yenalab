About

How this atlas is made

Map notes

Written from a geographer’s point of view: how the land formed (colliding plates, volcanoes, glaciers, river deposits) and where things are. Heights, lengths and areas are checked against government, international and academic sources, which are listed with each note.

Collection notes

Each entry is written from the point of view of its field: urban geography for cities, architectural history and archaeology for heritage, geology and landforms for nature, and animal ecology for animals. An easy version comes first, then the detailed one.

Pictures

The pixel art and illustrated maps were made by yenalab for the app. Coastlines and borders follow Natural Earth open data.

What we leave out

Political judgements and ongoing disputes between countries are not covered. History is kept short and limited to confirmed facts.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, please write to official@yenalab.com. We fix the app and this atlas together.
