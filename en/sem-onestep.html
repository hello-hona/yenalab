Sem OneStep

Learn math one handwritten step at a time.

How it works

Choose, write, and try again

01

Choose a unit

Pick a semester unit or comprehensive review for grades 1–6, then choose a difficulty level.

02

Work it out by hand

Use the scratch space for your working, then write your answer. A number keypad is available when handwriting recognition is difficult.

03

Review and retry

Revisit missed questions in the review notebook and check your practice days in the calendar and learning history.

Features

Made for everyday math practice

✍️

Handwriting recognition

Write digits and symbols in the answer box. You can select a recognition candidate or use the number keypad.

📚

Grade-level practice

Practice calculation-focused units from Korean elementary grades 1–6 by semester, then try comprehensive review.

💡

Step-by-step hints

Get guidance when you're stuck. Watching an ad may be required for additional hints.

📝

Review notebook

Collect missed questions and solve them again. Correctly answered questions leave the review list.

📅

Learning history

See practice days in a calendar and inspect session results and individual answers.

🍬

Jellies and customization

Earn jellies through practice and use them to personalize ink colors, pens, and your scratch space.
