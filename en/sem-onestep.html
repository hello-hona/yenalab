Features

Key features

Handwriting recognition grading

Digit and symbol recognition powered by Google ML Kit lets students solve problems by writing directly.

Full curriculum coverage

Study by unit across grades 1–6, organized into semester 1, semester 2, and comprehensive review.

AI word problems

Higher difficulty levels offer Gemini-generated word problems that build reasoning skills.

Review notebook and records

Wrong-answer review and a calendar-style learning record support a repeat-learning routine.
