Geography App

Where Is This?

A children's world geography exploration app where you pick a region on the world map and follow clues to discover hidden countries. Flags, capitals, a collection book, and passport stamps let you naturally revisit each country.

Features

Key features

World map exploration

Travel across continents and regions to find countries you have not yet discovered.

Clue-based discovery

Instead of showing the answer right away, location and flag hints guide you to identify each country.

Flag and capital quizzes

Review with quizzes: find the country from its flag, find the flag from its name, and guess the capital.

Collection book and passport stamps

Discovered countries can be revisited through the collection book, badges, and passport stamps as your exploration record grows.

On-device storage

Discovery records, quiz records, and settings are stored in the device's internal storage without sign-up.

Child-directed ad settings

Android ads are configured for child-directed treatment, a general-audience ad rating, and non-personalized ad requests.
