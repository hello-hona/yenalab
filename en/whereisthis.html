Where Is This?

A world geography adventure for kids: follow picture clues to find hidden countries. Pick a continent and region on the world map, discover a country from its clues, and its illustrated map and pixel-art collection open up. Flag and capital quizzes and a flag matching game help kids review.

Features

Features

Find countries from picture clues

Travel across continents and regions, and find each hidden country from picture clues and location hints instead of a ready answer.

An illustrated map for every country

All 37 countries have an illustrated map showing mountains, plains, rivers and islands. "Reading the land" notes explain how the land formed, with sources.

A pixel-art collection in 9 categories

City, heritage, nature, plants, culture, food, animals, greeting and flag, drawn for every country: 337 pictures, each with an easy and a detailed description.

Flag and capital quizzes, flag matching

Meet discovered countries again: find the country from its flag, the flag from its name, guess the capital, and match flags.

Collect passport stamps

Every discovery adds a passport stamp, so the whole journey can be seen at a glance.

Korean and English

Start in Korean or English and switch the language in settings at any time.

On-device storage

Discovery records, quiz records and settings are stored on the device, with no sign-up.

Child-directed ad settings

Android ads use child-directed treatment, a general-audience ad rating and non-personalized ad requests.
